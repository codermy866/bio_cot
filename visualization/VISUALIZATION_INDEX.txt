2. 在LaTeX中引用：

     \centering
     \includegraphics[width=0.6\textwidth]{ROC_Curves_Comparison.pdf}
     \caption{ROC curve comparison. Our Bio-COT 3.2 (red) achieves 
              AUC=0.8722, significantly outperforming baseline (blue, 
              AUC=0.75).}
     \label{fig:roc}
   \end{figure}

3. 或在Word中：插入→图片→选择PNG文件
